Immigrant Visa — Typical Required Documentation:

1. A valid passport.
2. Form DS-260, Immigrant Visa Electronic Application, confirmation page.
3. Two identical color photographs meeting official requirements.
4. Civil documents: birth certificate.
5. Civil documents: marriage certificate (if applicable) and evidence of
   termination of any prior marriages (if applicable).
6. Police certificates from all places lived since age 16.
7. Military records (if applicable).
8. Form I-864, Affidavit of Support, and supporting financial evidence
   from the sponsor.
9. Medical examination results from an authorized panel physician.
