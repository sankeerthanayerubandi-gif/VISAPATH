F-1 Student Visa — Typical Required Documentation:

1. A passport valid for travel to the United States.
2. Form I-20, "Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status,"
   issued by the SEVP-certified school, signed by both the student and a
   school official.
3. SEVIS I-901 fee payment receipt.
4. Form DS-160 confirmation page.
5. Application fee payment receipt.
6. One photograph meeting the official photo requirements.
7. Evidence of academic preparation (transcripts, diplomas, standardized
   test scores as required by the school).
8. Evidence of financial support sufficient to cover tuition and living
   expenses for the first year of study.
9. Evidence of intent to return home after completing studies.
