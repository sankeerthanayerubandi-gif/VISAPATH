B-1/B-2 Visitor Visa — Typical Required Documentation:

1. A passport valid for travel to the United States, with a validity date
   at least six months beyond the applicant's intended period of stay.
2. A completed nonimmigrant visa application, Form DS-160 confirmation page.
3. Application fee payment receipt.
4. One photograph meeting the official photo requirements.
5. Evidence of purpose of trip (e.g., business meeting invitation letter,
   tourism itinerary).
6. Evidence of intent to depart the United States after the visit
   (e.g., return ticket, ties to home country).
7. Evidence of ability to cover costs of the trip (e.g., bank statement,
   employer letter, sponsorship letter).
8. For business travel: a letter from the employer describing the purpose
   of travel.
